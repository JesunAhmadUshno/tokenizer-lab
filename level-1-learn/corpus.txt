### english
the tokenizer turns text into numbers. a model never sees letters, it sees token ids. frequent pairs of bytes merge into single tokens, so common words cost fewer tokens than rare words. the same sentence, tokenized two different ways, can cost twice as much to run. token counts decide context length, price, and speed. learning how a tokenizer is trained is learning where those costs come from.
the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. the dog does not mind. the fox runs again, and again the dog watches. repetition is what byte pair encoding feeds on: the more often a pair appears, the sooner it becomes one token.
